# MacRemote 1.1.3

MacRemote 1.1.3 makes an always-on Mac behave the same whether or not
MacRemote's window opened at login. It also includes 1.1.2's fix: a restart,
shutdown, or logout no longer waits on MacRemote's quit dialog.

## Download

Download `MacRemote-1.1.3.zip` from the Club post. It unpacks to a **MacRemote
1.1.3** folder with the app, the Club guide, these notes, and the source code.

MacRemote 1.1.3 needs macOS 26 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel
Macs. It is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens with
a double-click.

## Updating

Quit MacRemote, replace it in Applications with the new copy, and open it.
Your tools, connected apps, and settings carry over.

## What's fixed

### MacRemote works fully when it opens with no window

When macOS reopens apps after a restart, MacRemote comes back with no window
if its window was closed when the Mac went down. **Go public at launch** still
made your link public, but some of MacRemote's other work waited for a window
that never opened:

- Command-Q and **Quit MacRemote** quit without asking **Stop sharing and
  quit?**, and the next launch's Activity said MacRemote had restarted while
  your link was live.
- A new app asking to connect could not open the **Approval** window, and
  **Open MacRemote** in the menu bar could not open the main window.
- Notifications, including "Your public link is live", did not appear.
- MacRemote did not look for new MCP servers until you opened its window.

All of this now starts when MacRemote opens, window or not. The Approval
window and **Open MacRemote** need the menu bar icon after a launch with no
window, so keep **Show menu bar icon** on for an always-on Mac.

### Restarts no longer wait for the quit dialog (from 1.1.2)

macOS tells an app why it is quitting. MacRemote now quits quietly when the
Mac is restarting, shutting down, or logging out, and the public link stops as
it does on any quit. Pressing Command-Q, or choosing **Quit MacRemote**, still
asks while **Warn before quitting while sharing** is on in **Settings ›
General**.
