# MacRemote 1.1.2

MacRemote 1.1.2 stops a restart, shutdown, or logout from waiting on
MacRemote's quit dialog.

## Download

Download `MacRemote-1.1.2.zip` from the Club post. It unpacks to a **MacRemote
1.1.2** folder with the app, the Club guide, these notes, and the source code.

MacRemote 1.1.2 needs macOS 26 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel
Macs. It is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens with
a double-click.

## Updating

Quit MacRemote, replace it in Applications with the new copy, and open it.
Your tools, connected apps, and settings carry over.

## What's fixed

### Restarts no longer wait for the quit dialog

While your public link was on, MacRemote asked **Stop sharing and quit?**
every time it was told to quit, including when the Mac was restarting,
shutting down, or logging out. The restart then stalled until someone clicked
**Quit**. On a Mac with nobody at it, a scheduled macOS update or a remote
restart sat there until you screen-shared in.

macOS tells an app why it is quitting. MacRemote now quits quietly when the
Mac is restarting, shutting down, or logging out, and the public link stops as
it does on any quit. Pressing Command-Q, or choosing **Quit MacRemote**, still
asks while **Warn before quitting while sharing** is on in **Settings ›
General**.
