# MacRemote 1.1.2

MacRemote 1.1.2 fixes sign-in for apps that send a `state` value containing a plus sign. This may be why signing in from Meta's Muse failed. Your tools, settings, and connected apps carry over from 1.1.1.

## Download

Download `MacRemote-1.1.2-macos-universal2.zip` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/viticci/MacRemote/releases). Unzip it, move **MacRemote** to Applications, and open it. The release also lists a `.sha256` checksum for the ZIP. MacRemote is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Fixed

- **Sign-in could fail after you approved it** ([#13](https://github.com/viticci/MacRemote/issues/13)). When an app signs in, it sends MacRemote a random `state` value, and MacRemote hands it back when it returns you to the app, so the app can match the reply to its request. MacRemote sent a `+` in that value as is, and web servers read a bare `+` in an address as a space. An app whose `state` contained a `+` got back a value it didn't recognize, and the sign-in failed after you had approved it. MacRemote now sends `+` as `%2B`. Apps whose `state` has no `+`, such as Claude and ChatGPT, see exactly the same reply as before. Thanks to Paul Robichaux for the report.
